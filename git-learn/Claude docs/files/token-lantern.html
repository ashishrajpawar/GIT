A door with the light on.

Your product already speaks in doors — this design takes the metaphor seriously. Every live token is black glass with warm light inside it. Pause it and the light cools. Close it and the light goes out, for good. The whole interface is built from that one idea: obsidian surfaces, a single gold that means live, and quiet everywhere else. Serif for the few large words, Inter for everything working. No monospace, no gradients-for-decoration, no colour that doesn't carry meaning.

The one rule: gold appears only where a door is live. Nothing else in the app is ever gold.

The whole system fits in three cards.

Colour
Obsidian #0C0D10 Glass #15161A Warm white #F5F3EF Live gold #E3B664 Ember #D08A4C Close red #E4574E
Ember is gold cooling — it marks doors that are about to close. Red exists only on the Close action and the strike-through. Nothing decorative is coloured.
Type
Newsreader for the few big words
Inter for everything working
Both free, both everywhere, neither exotic. Codes are Inter with wide tracking and tabular figures — they read as codes without looking like a terminal. Nothing below 12px, everything scales with the phone's text size.
Skeleton — unchanged from v7
Four tabs: Chats · Tokens · Circles · Calls. Seal line at the top, action button bottom-right with clearance above the tab bar, two-tap mint, confirm before closing, fingerprint before your number is shown. Everything we agreed — internet voice and video, no KYC, locked-to-first-device, vague notifications, real send states, offline handling, block-the-person — ships inside this skin.
