# Teacher Notes

Working scratchpad for the `teach` skill — preferences and observations picked
up in session that do not belong in a spec.

> **This file owns nothing.** It used to restate the student profile, the stack,
> the out-of-scope list, the platform differences and the sharing rule, all of
> which `CLAUDE.md` owns — and it carried its own copy of *how many lessons
> Ashish had done*, which is the single fact this project has been burned by
> twice. Two homes for that fact is how "Modules 1 and 2 complete" survived for
> months against a student on lesson 2.
>
> **Where things actually live:**
>
> | You want | Read |
> |---|---|
> | Student profile, stack, product rules, conventions | `CLAUDE.md` |
> | **How far the student has actually got** | Ask him, or read `progress.js` localStorage. Nowhere else |
> | Counts, coverage, verification | `PROGRESS.md` (generated) |
> | What is in flight | `SESSION.md` |
> | Why past decisions went the way they did | `HANDOFF.md` |
